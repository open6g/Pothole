    How to run a pilot drive
    1. Open the page over HTTPS (your GitHub Pages link). Mount the phone rigidly, camera facing the road, and plug it in.
    2. Tap Start drive before you set off and allow Motion, Location and Camera. Don’t touch the phone again until you’ve parked. Recording stops if the screen locks or you switch apps, so the page keeps the screen awake.
    3. After parking, tap Stop & review. Label each flag as a pothole or not, then page through the frames to tag potholes the detector missed.
    4. The score shows precision (flags that were real) and recall (real potholes that were caught). Change the settings and press Re-analyze to tune without driving again.
    5. Download the drive JSON to keep it. It includes the frames and your labels, so it can be several MB per 10 minutes.
    How detection works
    • A slow low-pass filter tracks gravity, so the phone can sit at any angle. Vertical acceleration is the reading projected onto gravity, minus 1 g.
    • A jolt is flagged when that value crosses the threshold above the minimum speed. The next 0.4 s sets the peak and gyro peak. Severity is the peak ÷ threshold: low under 1.4×, medium under 2×, high from 2×.
    • One small frame is saved every second for finding misses. Each flag also keeps a sharper frame from about 0.7 s before the jolt.
    • Simulate draws a fake road with potholes, speed bumps and cracked patches, so you can practise reviewing. It also knows where the real potholes were and scores your review against them.
