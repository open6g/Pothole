    Run it on your phone
    1. Phones only give a web page motion, GPS and camera access over HTTPS. Opening this file directly from your downloads usually won't work.
    2. Quickest: drag this file onto Netlify Drop (or put it on GitHub Pages) and open the HTTPS link on your phone. Rename it to index.html first.
    3. iPhone (Safari): tap Start drive, then allow Motion & Orientation, Location and Camera. Android (Chrome): allow Location and Camera.
    4. Mount the phone rigidly with the camera facing the road, plug it in, and keep this tab open. The page keeps the screen awake. Recording stops if you lock the phone or switch apps.
    5. A passenger taps Mark pothole at each real pothole. Those marks are what the detector is scored against. The driver should never touch the phone.
    How detection works
    • A slow low-pass filter tracks gravity, so the phone can sit at any angle. Vertical acceleration is the reading projected onto gravity, minus 1 g.
    • A jolt is flagged when that vertical value crosses the threshold while you are above the minimum speed. The next 0.4 s sets the peak and the gyro peak.
    • Severity is the peak divided by the threshold: low under 1.4×, medium under 2×, high at 2× or more.
    • Camera frames are captured every 0.3 s. A flag keeps the frame from about 0.7 s before the jolt, when the pothole was still ahead of the car.
    • Simulate generates a drive with potholes (which are auto-marked as ground truth), unmarked speed bumps, rough patches and stops. The jolt detector should catch most potholes and flag a few bumps as false alarms.
